Campus Safety Guidelines

This document summarizes basic campus safety rules for students and staff. Always carry your ID card and do not share it with anyone. Visitors must sign in at the main gate and wear a visitor badge at all times. Report suspicious activity to the security desk immediately.

Library Rules

The library is a quiet zone. Phones must be on silent mode. Food and drinks are not allowed inside the reading area. Books can be borrowed for 14 days and renewed once if no other requests exist.

Lab Usage Policy

Only authorized students may access the computer lab. Do not install software without permission. Save your work to personal storage before leaving. Log out after finishing your session.
